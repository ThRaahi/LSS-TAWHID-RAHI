We are pleased to offer you employment with Living Spring Solutions (Care & Training) Limited in the role of Support Worker.
1. Hours of Work and Remuneration
Your appointment is based on flexible working hours, and your weekly hours may vary in accordance with operational requirements and service user needs. You will be paid £ per hour, with monthly remuneration calculated strictly on the total number of hours worked within each pay period, as verified through our authorised recording systems.

Your employment is subject to a probationary period of three (3) months. During this period, your performance, conduct, attendance, and suitability for the role will be assessed.

3. Training and Professional Development
You are required to participate in all mandatory training relevant to your role, including but not limited to:
  • Safeguarding
  • Health and Safety
  • Medication Awareness
  • Positive Behaviour Support
  • Infection Prevention and Control
  • Any additional training identified as necessary for safe practice or regulatory compliance
Training may be delivered internally or externally. Completion of mandatory training is a condition of continued employment.
